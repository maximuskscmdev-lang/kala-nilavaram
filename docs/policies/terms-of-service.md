## 5. Real-identity-behind-pen-names policy

Every contributor chooses a display identity per post: real name, pen
name/handle, or fully anonymous. **Regardless of your choice, your real,
verified identity is always recorded internally** (see Privacy Policy,
Section 4). This is a condition of using the publishing, review, and
whistleblower features — anonymity from other users is not anonymity from
the platform, and it is not a way to evade responsibility for what you
post. Only Editors, Moderators, and Super Admins can look up your real
identity behind a pen name, and every lookup is logged.

## 6. Defamation and liability disclaimer for user-generated content

- Reviews, complaints, and opinion content reflect the views of the
  individual contributor, not Kala Nilavaram.
- We require identity verification and editorial review specifically to
  reduce the risk of false or defamatory content reaching publication, but
  we cannot independently verify the truth of every claim before
  publication (study content and lighter-touch items are post-hoc
  moderated).
- If you believe content about you is false or defamatory, use the
  takedown/appeals process below rather than pursuing self-help remedies
  against the poster.
- To the maximum extent permitted by Indian law, Kala Nilavaram's liability
  for user-generated content is limited to our role as an intermediary
  under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its Rules, provided we
  comply with applicable due-diligence and takedown obligations.

## 7. Takedown and appeals process

1. Anyone (subject of a review/complaint/article, or any user) can flag
   content via the in-app report button, or email
   [legal@kalanilavaram.com] with the content link and reason.
2. The editorial/moderation team reviews flagged content within [5]
   business days and may: leave it up, edit it (with a visible edit note),
   or remove it.
3. The original author is notified of any removal and may appeal once,
   with additional context, to a different member of the editorial team
   than the one who made the original decision.
4. For legally mandated takedowns (court order, statutory notice), we will
   act per the timelines required under the IT Act, 2000 and its Rules.

## 8. School reviews and whistleblower reports specifically

- Reviews and complaints must reflect the genuine experience of a verified

- Teacher reviewers may only rate their school's administration,
  management, facilities, and working environment — not named colleagues.
- Submitting a knowingly false whistleblower report is a violation of
  these Terms and may result in account termination; it does not, however,
  change our approach of verifying reports respectfully and confidentially
  before any conclusion is drawn.
