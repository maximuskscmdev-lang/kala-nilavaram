## 1. Who we are

Kala Nilavaram ("we," "us," "the platform") is an independent, student-run
platform for school transparency, welfare, and community. We are not
affiliated with, controlled by, or answerable to any individual school's
management. This policy explains what we collect, why, how it's protected,
and what rights you have over it — for both student users (who are
typically minors under Indian law) and teacher users (adults).

## 2. What we collect

### 2.1 Account information (all users)
Real name, phone number, email (if using Google Sign-In), school
affiliation, role (student/teacher), and — for students — grade level.
**This real identity is always captured, even if you choose to publish
under a pen name or anonymously.** See Section 4 for why.

### 2.2 Content you submit
News articles, study materials, reviews, comments, event posts, and any
files/images you upload.

### 2.3 Whistleblower / complaint submissions
Category, description, optional evidence files, and your **name and
phone/email**, submitted so a moderator can verify and follow up with you.
This is the most sensitive category of data we hold — see Section 5.

### 2.4 Automatically collected
Basic usage/device data (for security and abuse prevention), and — if you
enable them — push notification tokens.

### 2.5 What we do **not** collect
We do not buy or import contact lists, and we do not track you across other
websites or apps for advertising purposes. We do not run third-party ad
networks.

## 3. Legal basis and minors (DPDP Act, 2023)

Most of our users are between 15 and 18 years old and are legally minors
under Indian law and under the DPDP Act's definition (anyone under 18).
Under the DPDP Act:

- We process minors' personal data only for purposes reasonably necessary
  to provide the platform's core functions (identity verification, content
  moderation, safety, and the features you've explicitly chosen to use).
- We do not undertake tracking, behavioral monitoring, or targeted
  advertising directed at minors.
- Where the DPDP Act or its rules require **verifiable parental/guardian
  consent** for specific processing activities involving users under 18,
  we will implement a consent flow before that processing begins — this is
  a **known open item pending final legal review**, given the platform's
  age band (15–18) sits close to thresholds that may be treated
  differently under forthcoming DPDP Rules. Get explicit legal sign-off on
  whether/how parental consent applies to this age band before launch.
- Teacher (adult) personal data is processed on the basis of consent given
  at signup and legitimate platform interest (verifying employment,
  operating the review and whistleblower systems).

## 4. Pen names, anonymity, and real-identity retention

You may choose to display your posts, reviews, or complaints under your
real name, a pen name/handle, or as fully anonymous. **Whichever you
choose, your real identity is always stored internally** — this is
required so that:

- Editorial and moderation teams can be held accountable for what gets
  published.
- We can respond to legitimate defamation or legal claims about specific
  content.
- Whistleblower reports can be verified as genuine before any action is
  taken.

Only Editors, Moderators, and Super Admins can look up the real identity
behind a pen name or anonymous post — and **every such lookup is
automatically logged** (who looked, when, and why), per our internal audit
trail.

## 5. Whistleblower / complaint data — special handling

- Your contact details submitted with a complaint are **encrypted at rest**
  and are never shown to other students, teachers, or the school itself.
- Only a small, named Moderation Team can decrypt and view this
  information, and every access is audit-logged.
- If a complaint is later referenced publicly (e.g., in an aggregated
  "issues this month" summary), it is fully de-identified first — no names,
  no identifying detail — by the editorial team.
- You can request deletion of your identifying contact information once
  your case is closed (see Section 7, Retention).
- We do not share whistleblower submitter identities with school
  management under any circumstance, except where legally compelled (see
  Section 9).

## 6. Who can access your data

| Data | Who can see it |
|---|---|
| Published content (news, reviews, study material) | Public |
| Real identity behind a pen name/anonymous post | Editor/Moderator/Super Admin of your school, logged access only |
| Whistleblower contact details | Assigned Moderators for your school + Super Admin, logged access only |
| Account info (phone, email) | You, and platform staff for account support |

Teachers **never** have moderator/editor access to the whistleblower inbox
or to reviews about their own school or colleagues, to avoid conflicts of
interest (see the Content Moderation Policy).

## 7. Retention and deletion

- **Account data**: retained while your account is active, deleted within
  [90] days of account deletion request, except where retention is needed
  for an open moderation/legal matter.
- **Whistleblower contact details**: retained only as long as needed to
  process your report. You may request deletion once a case is marked
  closed; absent a request, we default to purging identifying contact
  details [12 months] after closure, keeping only the de-identified case
  record for institutional accountability tracking.
- **Published content**: retained indefinitely as part of the platform's
  public record, unless removed via a takedown request (see Terms of
  Service) or a legal requirement.

## 8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act and as a matter of platform policy, you can:
- Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate personal data.
- Withdraw consent and request account/data deletion (subject to Section
  7's exceptions for open moderation matters and public content).
- Request deletion of whistleblower-submission contact details once your
  case is closed.
- Nominate a **Consent Manager** or lodge a grievance with our Grievance
  Officer (Section 10), and subsequently with the Data Protection Board of
  India, per the DPDP Act.

To exercise any of these, contact us at [privacy@kalanilavaram.com].

## 9. Legal disclosure

We may disclose personal data if required by a valid Indian legal process
(court order, law enforcement request under applicable law), or where we
believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to prevent imminent harm
to a person's safety. We do not disclose whistleblower submitter identities
to school management under any circumstance, including legal requests
directed at us by a school (a submitter's identity may still be disclosed
in response to a valid law-enforcement or court order directed at us,
independent of any school request).
